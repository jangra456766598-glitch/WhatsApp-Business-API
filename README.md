## What Is WhatsApp Business API?

The **WhatsApp Business API** allows medium and large businesses to send messages, automate responses, and manage communication at scale.  
Unlike the regular WhatsApp Business App, the API integrates with CRMs, chatbots, and marketing automation tools to enhance business communication.

### Key Benefits:
- 98% message open rate  
- Two-way customer communication  
- Chatbot integration for 24/7 support  
- E-commerce and CRM integration  
- Secure and verified messaging
